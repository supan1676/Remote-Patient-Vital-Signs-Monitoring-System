A reference, not a diagnosis.

Each signal gets its own typical range after seven days with enough valid recordings. Future readings are compared with that fixed range.

  • Missing readings are excluded.
  • Short bursts don’t count as full days.
  • The absent temperature sensor can join later.
How comparison works

The center is the median of sampled-minute averages. The range uses median absolute deviation with a small engineering noise floor. Three consecutive departures create an observation. This is a prototype statistical model, not a validated health assessment.
