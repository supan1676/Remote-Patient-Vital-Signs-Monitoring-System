# MAX30102 display and dashboard repair — 2026-11-03

## Outcome

**The missing-readings software fault is fixed and firmware `rpm-2.1.1` is
uploaded to COM3. The user confirmed that both the OLED and dashboard show
heart-rate/SpO2 numbers. Measurement accuracy and stability remain unvalidated.**

The dashboard is served by the real serial bridge at
<http://localhost:8080/index.html>. No demonstration readings were injected into
the live dashboard or database.

## Reproduced fault and cause

With a finger covering the illuminated sensor, the original firmware produced
strong red/IR signals (approximately 100,000/140,000 counts) but remained in
`warming_up`: **44 finger-present observations produced no valid heart rate or
SpO2** during the approximately 45-second before-fix capture.

The UART default had no software TX buffer. A 3–4 KB telemetry line at 115200
baud takes approximately 260–350 ms to transmit. Printing it synchronously every
second blocked acquisition longer than `OPTICAL_SAMPLE_GAP_MS = 150`. The next
loop cleared the FIFO and reset the optical window, preventing the 100 samples
required by the algorithm from accumulating normally.

## Changes

- Configure an **8,192-byte UART TX ring buffer before `Serial.begin()`** so
  telemetry transmission can proceed while the main loop services the sensor.
  Baud remains 115200 and optical/contact/quality thresholds are unchanged.
- Publish optical sample, completed-window and gap-reset counters in
  `sensor_health.max` to distinguish acquisition failures from poor contact.
- Identify the uploaded firmware as `rpm-2.1.1`.
- Correct application integer-format warnings under the fresh compiler.
- Align the bridge's optical perfusion-ratio limit with the firmware's
  100-sample peak-to-peak/mean calculation. Ratios above 20 no longer cause an
  otherwise valid packet, including SOS fields, to be discarded. Invalid types,
  negative ratios, non-finite values and ratios above the mathematical bound are
  still rejected. Added two regression tests for this contract.

The UART fix removes the recurring once-per-second sampling interruption; it
does not disable the gap guard. A later, separate TMP117 address-NACK/hot-plug
recovery at approximately uptime 409–413 s caused one **698 ms** acquisition
pause because the existing TMP117 initialization waits 600 ms. The guard
correctly reset the optical window once. This occurred without finger contact;
optical measurement subsequently resumed. The intermittent TMP117 problem
therefore remains open and can briefly interrupt optical acquisition during
recovery. It is distinct from the former permanent `warming_up` fault.

## Measurement limits

Successful display and transport do **not** establish correct clinical values.
The captured algorithm-valid estimates varied widely during placement/removal:
HR **31–214 bpm**, SpO2 **3–100%**. These are unvalidated device estimates, not
reference measurements or verified physiological changes. The existing validity
and quality checks do not establish accuracy; placement, artifact rejection and
comparison against a reference pulse oximeter remain necessary. No offset,
fabricated value, forced normal reading or smoothing was added to hide this
variation.

MPU remains offline; this repair does not establish fall detection.
